# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Germans aged 45–70 with chronic lower back pain. They've tried physiotherapy, exercises, and home remedies with limited results. They arrive tired of the cycle: pain, treatment, temporary relief, pain again. They use smartphones and tablets at home. They want trustworthy, calm, medically credible content — not gadget hype. They make considered purchases and need to feel the brand behind a product is serious.

## Product Purpose
Sell the Wellenpuls LWS: a neuro-muscular electrical stimulation (NMES) belt targeting deep lumbar stabilizer muscles that conventional therapy cannot reach. Single one-time purchase at 489€, with free bonuses (1:1 coaching call with the founder, video course, e-book). The goal is conversion earned through credibility and clarity, not urgency or pressure.

## Brand Personality
Calm confidence. Scientific honesty. Personal warmth.

## Anti-references
- EMS abs gadgets: too sporty, too "gym aesthetic"
- German Teleshopping / As Seen on TV: cheap, untrustworthy
- Cold medical device manufacturer sites: clinical, no humanity
- Fitness supplement brands (aggressive countdown timers, "LIMITED STOCK" red banners everywhere)

## Design Principles
1. Credibility before conversion. Every design choice earns trust first, then nudges action.
2. Calm hierarchy. The page guides, not shouts. One clear message per fold.
3. Evidence over claims. Show the science and the person behind the product.
4. Effortlessness for the reader. The 58-year-old should never work to understand this.
5. Warmth without informality. Founder-led and personal, but never casual or chatty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. Body text minimum 16px. CTA buttons minimum 52px height. No color-only encoding. Line length capped at 68ch.
